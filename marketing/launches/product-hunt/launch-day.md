# PH launch day

Product Hunt days run **12:01 AM to 11:59 PM Pacific**. Post at **12:01 AM PT** to bank a
full day of ranking. Ranking is votes + comments + engagement velocity, weighted early.

"T" = 12:01 AM PT.

| Time | Do |
|------|-----|
| T-1 day | Gallery uploaded, tagline final, first comment drafted, description in. Ask a few people who'd genuinely care to look in the morning (never "please upvote"). |
| T-12h | Confirm the [live site](https://parra-inc.github.io/feedback-mcp/) and repo load cold. |
| T-0 (12:01 AM PT) | Launch goes live. Immediately post the [maker's first comment](first-comment.md). |
| T+0 to morning | Notify your own channels it's live, with the link. "We're live on PH today" + link is fine; "please upvote" is not. |
| Morning-midday PT | **Peak window.** Reply to every comment fast. Answer the question you asked back to them. |
| All day | Stay present. Thank hunters and commenters. Keep the thread warm. |
| Evening | Last push to any owned channels in other timezones. |
| T+1 day | Follow-up (see [`follow-up.md`](follow-up.md)). |

## What wins on PH

- A gallery that shows the product working (the [`gallery.md`](gallery.md) order is built
  for this; frame 1 is the thumbnail and does 80% of the work).
- A warm, human maker comment with a real question, and you replying to everyone.
- Being present the entire day, not just the first hour.

## What loses

- Asking for upvotes (against the rules, easy to detect).
- A static gallery where a demo video belonged. **Add the rendered demo video as gallery
  item #2** (see [`../demo-video.md`](../demo-video.md) / `/demo-video`).
- Launching and disappearing.

## Comment handling

Same spirit as HN but warmer. The recurring one will again be **"why no dashboard?"**, so
answer it as the intentional design choice it is, not a missing feature. For "is this like
Canny/Sleekplan?", be gracious: your angle is self-hosted, own-your-data, no per-seat
pricing, AI-native read layer. Never trash a competitor.

## Reframe from HN

This is not a copy-paste of the HN launch. HN got the architecture and the trade-offs;
Product Hunt gets the story and the outcome. Warmer voice, benefit-first, a question that
invites replies. If HN produced any "I self-hosted this" comments, they're testimonials
you can quote here.
