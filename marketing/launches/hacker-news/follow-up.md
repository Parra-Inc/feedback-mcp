# HN follow-up (T+24h)

Do this while it's fresh. A launch you don't harvest is half-wasted.

## Capture

- **What ranked and when.** Peak rank, time on front page, which title. Note it for the
  next launch.
- **What people asked for.** Every recurring request is roadmap and future content. Likely
  themes: "give me a dashboard option," "add a widget," "Postgres row-level multi-tenancy,"
  "delete as an MCP tool," "Mongo." Turn each into a GitHub issue.
- **Who showed up.** Thank thoughtful commenters. Note potential contributors, users, and
  anyone who said "I'll self-host this."
- **The numbers,** against the one goal: GitHub stars, repo traffic (GitHub Insights),
  clones, and actual deploys if you can see them.

## Thank

- Reply once more to the sharpest critical thread with "shipped/filed this, thanks for the
  push" if you acted on it. Closing the loop publicly is worth more than the original post.
- DM or @ the 2-3 people who engaged most genuinely.

## Feed the next thing

The best exchange in the thread is your next piece of content. Strong candidates from this
project:

- A blog post: **"Why I gave my feedback tool an MCP server instead of a dashboard."**
  The design bet is the whole story, and it's the Product Hunt reframe too.
- A short writeup: **"One Prisma schema across Postgres, SQLite, and Cloudflare D1."** Pure
  HN catnip, and it's already built.
- A writeup of the **stateless MCP OAuth 2.1 flow** (HMAC over a single secret, PKCE,
  dynamic registration). People are actively figuring out MCP auth right now.

## Then: Product Hunt

If HN went well, carry the momentum to Product Hunt on Day 4 (see
[`../product-hunt/`](../product-hunt/)). Any "I self-hosted this and it's great" comment
from HN is a testimonial you can point to.
